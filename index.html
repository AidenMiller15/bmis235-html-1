Running in Spokane

A page about the best places to go for a run in Spokane

I have run all over the world andSpokane is one of the best places to go! These are some of the nicest places to go out for a run.

Spokane hosts one of the largest road races in the world, The Lilac Bloomsday race, hostingover 40000 runners in 2026..Many coming from all over the world to compete.

Three best places to run in Spokane

  • James T. Slavin Conservation Area: With dirt roads stretching for miles and beautiful trails for low impact running.
  • Bowl and Pitcher: Beautiful views, and trails that that circle riverside state park. One of the best spots for great scenery.
  • Centennial Trail: 40 miles of paved trail with beautiful views, great communities, and changes in elevation.
